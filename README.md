# The World Today — Thursday 26 November 2026

A newspaper front page summing up the world's news of Thursday 26 November
2026 in one image (1024x1024 PNG). Lead story: **Tariff shock rocks world
markets** — Washington proposes new country-specific tariffs on goods from
14 major trading partners, with an extra 20% on imports from China, as the
AI rout deepens (Nasdaq −2%, Seoul −6%, Tokyo −3%, the VIX fear gauge up
60% in November, chip stocks halved from June's record). Also covered: the
first Pakistani astronaut reaching China's Tiangong space station aboard
Shenzhou-24; the Japan–Europe BepiColombo probe reaching Mercury after
eight years; Colombia marking ten years since its peace agreement; the
Australia–Solomon Islands security treaty; Bangladesh garment workers'
36% wage rise; US markets closed for Thanksgiving; and Suez Canal revenues
more than doubling.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` byte-for-byte
to the output path and verifies the copy, so two runs produce identical
output. Sources for every story are listed in `SOURCES.md`.
